<Audience>Administrators</Audience>

Admins keep the workspace healthy: confirm new instruments, watch the fleet, issue tokens, manage who is an admin, roll out watcher releases, and connect Linear and Slack. Admins can also delete any run comment and review the feedback people send about Data Hub.

The admin-only pages under **Settings** are **Feedback**, **Integrations**, **Watchers**, and **Members**. Members don’t see them in the settings menu.

Roles and scopes are defined in [Security and permissions](/docs/security). Day-to-day operator setup is in [Set up an instrument](/docs/set-up-an-instrument).
