<Audience>Administrators</Audience>

Members sign in with Google. Admins decide who else has the admin role. [Security and permissions](/docs/security#roles) lists what members and admins can each do.

## Open the members list

Go to **Settings → Members**. The settings menu hides this page from members, and a member who opens its URL sees an **Admins Only** message.

The table lists everyone who has signed in, with their name, email, role, and an **Admin** switch.

![Settings Members page with the workspace members table and Admin toggles](/images/workspace-members.webp)

## Promote or demote

Turn **Admin** on or off on a row to give or remove admin access, then confirm. Data Hub checks the role again on every admin action, so the change applies from that person’s next one. The admin-only buttons and sidebar entries they see can take up to 5 minutes to change, because they come from their [cached session](/docs/security#session-lifetime).

You can’t demote yourself. That rule stops the last admin from removing their own admin role. Ask another admin to demote you if you need to.

## Choose the first admins [#first-admin-bootstrap]

The first admins come from the `ADMIN_EMAILS` environment variable, a comma-separated list of email addresses that ignores case. Data Hub makes each listed person an admin every time they sign in. After that, any admin can promote others from this page.

People appear on this page after they first sign in to your deployment, and Data Hub has no invitations. Any Google account that your Google OAuth client accepts can sign in. That client, which your team created when it set up Data Hub, decides who appears here. See [Who can sign in](/docs/security#who-can-sign-in).
