---
title: qPCR runs show melting curves and the PDF report
---

Azure Cielo qPCR runs now show the instrument’s PDF report under **Report Data**. A run with more than one PDF gets previous and next buttons. A new **Melting Curves** section shows the plate for one dye channel at a time.

Click a well to see its melting curve and derivative, with a dashed line at the melting temperature. To add these sections to an older run, reprocess it.
