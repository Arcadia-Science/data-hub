---
title: Slack notifications show text exactly as typed
---

Slack messages from Data Hub now show comment text, names, run IDs, and dispatched messages exactly as they were typed. Text that looks like a Slack link, such as `<https://example.com|label>`, or a mention, such as `@here` or `<!channel>`, no longer becomes a link or a mention. This applies to new-run messages in the Slack channel and to direct messages about runs, comments, notifications, and feedback. The "View in Data Hub" link still works.
