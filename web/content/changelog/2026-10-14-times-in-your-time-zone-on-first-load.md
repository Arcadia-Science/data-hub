---
title: Dates and times show in your time zone from the first moment
---

Dates and times on run pages, instrument pages, watcher pages, the files table, and the notification list used to load in UTC and then jump to your time zone a moment later. They now load in your time zone right away.

The watcher page also decides what "today" means in your time zone, so its default date range no longer changes near midnight UTC.
