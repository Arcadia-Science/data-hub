---
title: DishCam runs play as a video
---

Open a DishCam run to watch its photos as a video at the top of the report. You can play, pause, and drag through the timeline, and a still frame shows before you press play. The run also saves the camera settings from its `run.json` file, such as the photo count and capture speed.

Videos play at 10 photos per second or faster, so slow captures stay watchable. Admins can now choose DishCam as an instrument type.
