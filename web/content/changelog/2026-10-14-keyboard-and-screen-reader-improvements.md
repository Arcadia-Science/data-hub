---
title: Plate maps, charts, and tooltips work with a keyboard and a screen reader
---

Plate maps and Aunty and qPCR plates are now one stop on the Tab key. Use the arrow keys to move between wells, Home and End to jump along a row, and Enter to open a well. Each well reads out its name, and plate map wells read out their value, so you no longer need to hover to see it.

Charts for Raman spectra, qPCR melting curves, Aunty curves, and watcher heartbeats now come with a short text description of their range and peak. The connectivity strip on the watcher Status tab can be stepped through with the arrow keys.

Run status icons now say their status in text. Tooltips on file patterns, watched folders, metadata overflow badges (+N), and file status labels open from the keyboard. Buttons that are unavailable, such as Upload while the watcher is offline, can be focused so the reason shows.

Dialogs and menus no longer scroll the page behind them on touch screens. People who set their system to reduce motion no longer see dialogs zoom or loading placeholders pulse. Dark mode now also darkens browser scrollbars and form controls. Screen readers announce new notifications, and loading and error messages in chart windows and previews.
