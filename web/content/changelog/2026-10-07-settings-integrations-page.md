---
title: Slack channel setting moved to Settings → Integrations
docs: https://datahub.arcadiascience.com/docs/configure-notifications
---

Workspace admins now manage connections that the whole workspace uses on a new page, **Settings → Integrations**. It shows in the Settings menu for admins only.

The **Slack channel** setting, which holds the webhook that posts a message when a new run is created, moved there from **Settings → Notifications**. The form works the same way, and any saved webhook keeps working.

**Settings → Notifications** now holds personal choices only, including your own Slack connection for direct messages.
