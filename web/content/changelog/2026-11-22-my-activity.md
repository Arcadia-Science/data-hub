---
title: My runs is now My activity and lists your lab notebooks
docs: https://datahub.arcadiascience.com/docs/browse-runs#find-a-run
---

**My runs** in the sidebar is now **My activity**. Each person's page lists the lab notebooks they own below their runs, and its **Runs today** card is now **Lab notebooks this week**.

Home lists the lab notebooks that got a run today, with a date picker and **Name** and **Owner** column filters.

On Home, My activity, and a lab notebook's page, the **Run ID** and **Instrument** column headers replace the search box and **Instruments** button. The headings read **Instrument runs**, **Lab notebooks**, and **Comments**. The sidebar remembers whether **Instruments** and **Lab Notebooks** are open.
