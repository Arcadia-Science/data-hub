---
title: Large DishCam and Hina Microscope stacks now process
---

DishCam and Hina Microscope stacks that used to fail as too large to process now process.

Processing has a 15-minute limit. If Data Hub can’t finish a stack in that time, the stack shows **Failed**. Its message says how far through the frames it got. You can still download the raw file.

A Hina Microscope image too large to preview also shows **Failed**, and you can still download the raw file. So does an image from a color camera, which Data Hub can't preview yet.
