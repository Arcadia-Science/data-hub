---
title: Recent comments appear on the home page, profiles, and a new Comments page
---

The home page shows up to four of today’s comments, and a date picker changes the range. Each profile shows recent comments the person wrote, or comments on runs they ran.

**View all comments** opens the new Comments page, which lists every comment by day. Filter it to comments on your runs, or by instrument. Each comment links to its place on the run page.
