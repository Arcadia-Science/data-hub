---
title: Instrument and watcher dialogs say why a change failed instead of crashing the page
---

If a change from an instrument's **Edit details**, **Retire** or **Reactivate** dialog failed, the page showed "Something Went Wrong" instead of the reason. The same happened in a watcher's **Deregister** dialog. A change fails like this when, for example, your session has expired or you're no longer an admin. These dialogs now show the reason in a message and stay open.
