---
title: Each lab notebook has a page with its runs from every instrument
docs: https://datahub.arcadiascience.com/docs/lab-notebooks#notebook-page
---

A lab notebook's name now opens its page in Data Hub, which lists the lab notebook's runs from every instrument, with its description and owner. **Open lab notebook** opens the lab notebook itself, and **Copy page link** copies the page's address, so you can paste it into the lab notebook. Use **Edit details** to rename a lab notebook or change its description, owner, or link, and **Delete** to take it off its runs. A deleted lab notebook's page offers **Restore**, which brings back all of its runs.

**Lab Notebooks** in the sidebar lists the lab notebooks you opened last, and **View all** lists every lab notebook with its owner, run count, instruments, and newest run. Choose **New lab notebook** there to create one before it has any runs.
