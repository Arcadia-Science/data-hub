---
title: Each lab notebook has a page with its runs from every instrument
docs: https://datahub.arcadiascience.com/docs/browse-runs#notebook-page
---

A lab notebook's name now opens its page in Data Hub, which lists the notebook's runs from every instrument, with its description and owner. **Open notebook** opens the notebook itself, and **Copy page link** copies the page's address, so you can paste it into the notebook. Use **Edit details** to rename a notebook or change its description, owner, or link, and **Delete** to take it off its runs. A deleted notebook's page offers **Restore**, which brings back all of its runs.

**Notebooks** in the sidebar lists the notebooks you opened last, and **View all** lists every notebook with its owner, run count, instruments, and newest run. Choose **New notebook** there to create one before it has any runs.
