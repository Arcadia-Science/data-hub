---
title: Open instruments, watchers, search results, and table pages in a new tab
---

Instrument and watcher names in the lists are now links, so you can Cmd-click, Ctrl-click, or middle-click them to open them in a new tab. Search results work the same way, and so do the page numbers under a table. The Previous and Next arrows no longer respond to the keyboard when there is no earlier or later page.

Keyboard users also get a "Skip to Main Content" link as the first stop on every page, a visible outline on the comment box, and a page title that screen readers can find on the dashboard and every settings page.
