---
title: Open instruments, watchers, search results, and table pages in a new tab
---

Instrument and watcher rows in the lists are now links, so you can Cmd-click, Ctrl-click, or middle-click a row to open it in a new tab. Search results work the same way, and so do the page numbers under a table. The Previous and Next arrows no longer respond to the keyboard when there is no earlier or later page.

Keyboard users also get a "Skip to Main Content" link as the first stop on every page, which jumps past the sidebar and the header buttons. The comment box shows an outline when it has focus, and screen readers can find a page title on the dashboard and every settings page.
