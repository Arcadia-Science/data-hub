Data Hub now reads exports from the Unchained Labs Aunty, which measures protein melting temperature and particle size. The run page shows a plate with a small chart for each well, and clicking a well opens its full chart.

Data Hub reads thermal ramps, sizing runs, isothermal holds, and exports without curves. Filter Aunty runs by experiment type, analysis mode, temperature, and ramp rate.
