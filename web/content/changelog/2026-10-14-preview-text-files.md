---
title: Preview CSV, JSON, and other text files without downloading them
docs: https://datahub.arcadiascience.com/docs/browse-runs#download-files
---

Click a CSV, TSV, JSON, or other text file's row in the files table to read it in a window on the page. CSV and TSV files show as a table, and JSON is formatted so it is easier to read. Large files show their first 1 MB, and the window says so.

Use the back and forward buttons in the window to move through the other previewable files on the same page of the table.
